# DeployCo template profile

Load this profile only when the current presentation brief selects DeployCo branding
or an approved DeployCo template. It constrains style, not story mode: a DeployCo deck
may be technical sharing, proposal, training, status, or another user-defined flow.

## Source of truth

Use the actual DeployCo Slide Template Master or an explicitly approved DeployCo deck
containing that master. Do not recreate the theme from memory, sample a brand from a
screenshot, synthesize custom backgrounds, or substitute Tomoro colors.

Before editing, record:

- template/deck path or shared-deck source in task-local provenance;
- master and layout names used by cover, content, section, and closing slides;
- slide aspect ratio;
- theme font families and representative content-title size/style;
- theme colors sampled from the master, including charcoal, cyan, mint, lavender,
  neutral backgrounds, rules, and accessible text colors;
- approved logos, icons, and asset restrictions.

If provenance cannot be established, stop DeployCo-styled production. Report the
missing template or asset, affected slides, and exact input needed. A clearly
provisional, brand-neutral storyboard may continue when content evidence is adequate.

## Visual register

- Favor light or white canvas and native DeployCo backgrounds/layouts.
- Use template charcoal for primary text and structure. Use cyan, mint, and lavender
  only as sampled from the active template; remembered hex values are not authority.
- Use the modern sans-serif family defined by the theme. Reserve monospace for exact
  commands, contracts, paths, or code-shaped evidence.
- Keep one consistent title size, position, and wrapping behavior across content
  slides. Allow explicit cover and closing exceptions.
- Preserve master margins, guides, footers, page numbering, and logo clear space.
- Prefer thin precise rules/connectors. Avoid gradients, shadows, glow, bevels, 3D,
  pseudo-isometric infrastructure, decorative artwork, and cartoon styling unless a
  newer explicit user direction overrides the profile.

## Editability and preservation

- Keep titles, subtitles, labels, explanatory text, values, callouts, footers, simple
  cards, status chips, and connectors native and editable.
- Do not flatten a whole slide or bake narrative text into a background image.
- Work on a copy and keep the known-good source untouched.
- Do not replace the master, globally reset slides, or relink every slide to a generic
  layout without a before/after visual review.
- Do not overwrite proven diagram files. Create a versioned successor and update one
  centralized source mapping.
- Do not stretch images. Use proportional crop or white padding.
- When import/export changes the master or theme, return to the approved template and
  reapply the correct layout instead of compensating with custom backgrounds.

## DeployCo technical-diagram register

When both this profile and technical-sharing mode are active:

- use a light/white canvas with template charcoal, cyan, mint, and lavender roles;
- use modern sans-serif, consistent hierarchy, thin directed connectors, and short
  exact labels;
- make diagrams prove a state transition, authority boundary, failure path, coherent
  topology, rollback, or feedback loop;
- avoid paragraphs inside diagrams, gradients, shadows, 3D/cartoon styling, and
  generic boxes-and-arrows without a decision.

This diagram register is a DeployCo-specific default, not a global restriction on
other templates or user-directed styles.
